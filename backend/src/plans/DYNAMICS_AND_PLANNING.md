# Aegis Dynamic Stratification & Income-Aware Planning

## Overview
This document outlines the **"True Work"** orchestration behind Aegis's dynamic insurance stratification. Unlike static platforms that offer one-size-fits-all premiums, Aegis utilizes real-time worker dynamics to calibrate costs based on deterministic income snapshots.

## 1. Deterministic Weekly Earnings
Every gig worker in the Aegis ecosystem has a unique, deterministic financial profile generated by the **Dynamic Q-Commerce Engine**.
- **Deterministic Consistency**: Earnings are generated using a `DeterministicRandom` algorithm tied to the `internalDriverId` and `weekKey`. This ensures that a worker sees the same, stable income throughout the week—mirrored across both the backend and mobile dashboard.
- **Realistic Banding**: Income values are derived from actual market benchmarks (NITI Aayog & Fairwork India Ratings 2023), ensuring they are neither zero nor unrealistically high. Typical bands range from ₹3,500 to ₹15,000 per week.

## 2. Income-Aware Plan Stratification
The **Plans Service** analyzes the active earnings profile to suggest a triplet of coverage options (Basic, Standard, Elite).
- **Affordability Guardians**: The system applies three distinct pricing "floors" based on the worker's earnings band:
    - **Low Earnings ($< ₹5,000$)**: Plans are throttled down (e.g., ₹23.XX) to minimize economic strain.
    - **High Earnings ($> ₹10,000$)**: Plans scale up (e.g., ₹29.XX - ₹49.XX) to offer higher coverage tiers as per the worker's increased capacity.
- **Precision Pricing**: To avoid the psychological "Simplistic-Effect" of whole numbers, the engine utilizes a deterministic fractional generator to produce industrial-strength decimals (e.g., ₹34.82), enhancing perceived system authenticity and professionalism.

## 3. End-to-End Dynamic Wiring
- **Native Integration**: The logic is natively integrated into the `PlansController` and `PlansService`, consuming real life-cycle data from the `DynamicQCommerceModule`.
- **Actuarial Realism**: The `Ct` (Coverage Factor) is mapped to each tier, ensuring that the dynamic price remains actuarially sound relative to the underlying risk metrics.
