### The Triple-Partition Stratification Model
Unlike any other platform, Aegis does not treat premiums as generic revenue. Each INR injected into the ecosystem is surgically sliced into three deterministic buckets:

#### 1. The Core Risk Pool (80% Weight)
*   **The Mission**: Instantaneous, ultra-low latency payout execution.
*   **The Logic**: These funds are the operational fuel of the platform. They are liquid, unencumbered, and dedicated exclusively to parametric claims triggered by our environmental oracles (AQI, Heatwave, Monsoons).
*   **Mathematical Defensive**: By dedicating 80% to risk, we maximize worker payout capacity while maintaining operational overhead.

#### 2. The Contingency Reserve (15% Weight)
*   **The Mission**: Shielding the ecosystem against "Black Swan" climatic anomalies.
*   **The Logic**: These funds are locked behind an actuarial gate. They reside in a high-order state that is only tapped during extreme stress events where the Core Risk Pool is depleted.
*   **Strategic Supremacy**: This buffer allows Aegis to withstand a **5-Year Anomaly** (e.g., 85% of active drivers hitting payout thresholds simultaneously) without platform collapse.

#### 3. The Platform Sustainability Fee (5% Weight)
*   **The Mission**: Perpetual infrastructure hardening and development.
*   **The Logic**: A non-negotiable slice for tier-1 engineering maintenance. This ensures that the platform’s compute, GPS triangulation, and spatial H3 nodes are always operating at 100/100 performance levels.

### Deterministic Settlement Circuit Breaking
Our **Hardened Settlement Pipeline** integrates directly with the ARS engine. Before any payout is authorized, the system performs a **Zero-Trust Solvency Handshake**:

1.  **Core Check**: The system attempts to settle from the Core Risk Pool.
2.  **Panic-Buffer Activation**: If the Core is insufficient, the **ARS Fail-Safe** evaluates the Contingency Reserve.
3.  **Violent Rejection**: If total platform liquidity (Core + Contingency) cannot cover the debt, the system **"Fails-Closed"** immediately. We prioritize protecting the existing pool's integrity over imprecise, fractional settlements.
