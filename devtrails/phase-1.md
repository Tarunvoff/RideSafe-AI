# Guidewire DEVTrails 2026 - Phase 1 (Ideation & Foundation)

## Timeline
- Dates: March 4 - March 20 (Weeks 1-2)
- Submission deadline: March 20, End of Day
- Theme: Ideate & Know Your Delivery Worker

## Mandatory Guardrails (Must Be Followed)
- Persona must be delivery partners only:
  - Food delivery (example: Swiggy/Zomato)
  - E-commerce delivery (example: Amazon/Flipkart)
  - Grocery/Q-commerce delivery (example: Zepto/Blinkit)
- Coverage scope must be loss of income only.
- Strictly exclude health, life, accidents, and vehicle repair coverage.
- Financial model must be weekly pricing only.

## Problem Focus for the Solution
- External disruptions reduce worker earning hours and monthly income.
- Platform should protect gig workers from uncontrollable events (not physical damage costs).
- Indicative disruptions (teams should adapt for persona):
  - Environmental: heavy rain, flood, heat, pollution
  - Social/operational: curfews, zone closures, strikes, sudden local restrictions

## Phase 1 Deliverables

### 1) Idea Document (README in repository)
Must clearly include:
- Requirement interpretation with persona-based scenarios.
- End-to-end workflow of the platform.
- Weekly premium model design:
  - How pricing is calculated weekly
  - Why weekly model fits worker earning cycle
- Parametric trigger strategy:
  - Which events trigger claim initiation
  - Why those triggers are relevant for chosen delivery persona
- Platform choice justification:
  - Web vs Mobile and why
- AI/ML plan:
  - Dynamic premium calculation approach
  - Fraud detection approach
  - Any predictive models planned
- Tech stack and development roadmap.
- Any additional assumptions, constraints, and innovation points.

### 2) Repository Link
- Public or accessible GitHub/GitLab repository.
- Same repository must continue for Phase 2 and Phase 3.

### 3) 2-Minute Strategy Video
- Publicly accessible link.
- Must show:
  - Problem understanding
  - Execution plan
  - Early prototype/minimal scope for Phase 1

## Suggested Phase 1 Completion Checklist
- Persona finalized and justified.
- Loss-of-income-only boundaries clearly documented.
- Weekly pricing formula drafted with sample values.
- Trigger matrix created (event, threshold, data source, impact).
- Initial fraud strategy documented (high-level rules + model idea).
- UX flow drafted (onboarding -> policy -> trigger -> claim -> payout).
- Repository README updated with all above sections.
- Demo video recorded and uploaded.

## Exit Criteria for Phase 1
- Idea and architecture are clear enough for implementation.
- Weekly model and trigger logic are explicit and testable.
- Scope does not violate exclusions (health/life/accident/vehicle repair).
- Team is ready to build registration, policy, pricing, and claim automation in Phase 2.
